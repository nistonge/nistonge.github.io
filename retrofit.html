Professional work

Soft-story seismic retrofit

City Structural, Inc., San Francisco · Summer 2026 · Layout, Microsoft Excel

The work

Oakland's mandatory retrofit ordinance requires older buildings with soft story conditions to be strengthened in preparation for future seismic events. This sheet, taken out of a larger document, locates new shear walls, collectors, and blocking through an existing building. Using a provided template, I produced the plan sets and documentation issued for permitting and construction, ran the supporting design calculations, and checked the drawings against the governing code and ordinance requirements before they went out.

What I took from it

Although a template was used, each project differs greatly in the type and extent of retrofit required, therefore I was able to greatly improve my critical thinking skills and gained a deeper understanding of tradeoffs in development. I found the detail dependent aspect of this internship to be the most challenging. For example, adjusting a shear wall by just two feet had the potential to completely alter the shear and anchor forces, causing analysis to fail. By the end of this internship, I learned the importance of building a strong foundation, both literally and metaphorically, when planning a multi-step project like this one.
