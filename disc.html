El Cerrito BART redevelopment

Design and Innovation for Sustainable Cities (DISC), UC Berkeley · Summer 2025 · Rhinoceros, Adobe Illustrator

The work

DISC is a summer program with UC Berkeley CED where I worked with a team to create a proposal for transit-oriented redevelopment around the El Cerrito BART station. I produced the site analysis behind it, researching the water, environmental and energy systems the site would have to work with, and presented the concept in formal design reviews.

What the analysis drove

Spacing the buildings deliberately increased shade across the site and raised its albedo, which lowers the heat the development absorbs. Improving transit access and reducing parking space allowed us to integrate more green and community spaces. We also designed for a future with more automation and delivery robots, which lets more of the circulation be walkable paths instead of roads.
