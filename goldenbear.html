Golden Bear Computing

Cal Sustainable Solutions (ASCE), UC Berkeley · 2026 · 2nd Place, ASCE Nationals

The work

Each year the team responds to a mock request for proposal with a complete technical report and Envision justification. Golden Bear Computing was our 2026 submission and the prompt tasked our team to propose a design for a sustainable data center. I modeled the development in SketchUp, including the hydrogen fuel cells on the roof, the central community area, and the green walls. I also ran material calculations and cost estimating for the proposal, and calculated the energy and water consumption of the green walls.

A decision and a tradeoff

One challenge was working out where machinery would be placed. We wanted the solar panels on the roof alongside the hydrogen fuel cells, which would have kept generation close to the equipment it served and freed up ground area. The combined load was too great for the roofs, so we dedicated a section of the site to the panels instead. That buys a structure that works, but it costs usable land and isolates the panels.
